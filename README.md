# Website & App Builder by AI Research Plus

Plan a new website, web app, mobile app, online store, chatbot or AI agent with Claude in a few quick questions, then get a link that opens Emergent's AI app builder with your plan filled in.

## How it works

1. Tell Claude what you want to build.
2. Claude asks a few short questions, one at a time, and shows its progress as it goes.
3. Claude writes a short build brief in English and sends it, with a project name, to the AI Research Plus link builder.
4. You get a link. Opening it takes you to Emergent (emergent.sh), which may ask you to sign in or create an account.

Afterwards, Claude can help with the next steps, such as writing your page text or planning your launch.

## Setup

Install the plugin, then connect its link-builder connector: in Claude, open Customize > Plugins, select this plugin, and connect it on the Connectors tab. No sign-in is needed. In Claude Code, the connector loads with the plugin.

## Try it

- "I need a website for my bakery, Sweet Crumbs. Warm and rustic, with a menu and a contact page."
- "Make me a mobile app for my gym where members can book classes and see the schedule."
- "I want an online store for my handmade candles, with a product catalog, a cart and card checkout."

## Data

The plugin sends two things to the AI Research Plus link-builder server at airesearchplus.com: the project name and the build brief Claude wrote from your answers. The server uses them only to build the link, returns the link to the chat, and doesn't store them. If you open the link, Emergent receives the same project name and brief.

The link carries no affiliate or referral code, and the plugin shows no ads or sponsored content.
